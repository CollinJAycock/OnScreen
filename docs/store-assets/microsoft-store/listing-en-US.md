# Microsoft Store listing (English, United States)

Partner Center: OnScreen (Store ID 9N58M48F5XMW), device family Xbox.

## Description

OnScreen plays the media library on your own OnScreen Media Server, free open-source software you install on your own computer or NAS. The app includes no movies, shows, music or channels: connect it to your server to browse and play the videos, music, audiobooks and photos you have added there.

To use this app you need your own OnScreen Media Server set up and running, with media you have added to it. OnScreen does not provide, sell, host or link to any movies, shows, music or TV channels. It has no web browser, no add-ons and no way to open stream links or playlists; it works only with your OnScreen server. Please add only media you own or have the right to use.

On Xbox Series X|S, OnScreen plays your own 4K video in HDR10 and Dolby Vision on TVs that support them, and switches your TV into the right mode for each film.

Getting started:
1. Install OnScreen Media Server on a computer or NAS on your network (see github.com/CollinJAycock/OnScreen).
2. Add your own media to it.
3. Open this app, enter your server's address, and sign in.

No ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks only to the server you choose, and loads the images it points to (such as film posters). Privacy policy: https://onscreen.wolverscreen.com/privacy

## Product features

- Home screen with Continue Watching, Next Up and recently added items from your libraries
- Movies, TV shows, music, audiobooks and photos, with posters, descriptions and search
- 4K HDR10 and Dolby Vision on TVs that support them, with the TV switched to the right mode
- Plays HEVC, MKV and Dolby Digital Plus files from your server as they are
- Resume where you left off, with watch progress shared with your other OnScreen apps
- Audio track, subtitle and chapter choices, and Skip Intro / Skip Credits where your server has marked them
- Music and audiobooks with a now-playing screen and playback speed, and photo slideshows
- Designed for the Xbox controller
- Sign in with your username and password, or from your phone or computer with a PIN
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
