# OnScreen v2.6.0 (draft — not yet released)

The catch-up release. v2.6 closes most of the everyday gaps people notice
coming from Plex, Jellyfin or Emby — editing metadata, multi-episode files,
movie editions, label restrictions, smaller downloads, studio pages, instant
mixes, an e-reader catalog — and brings history over from those servers. The
server's play decision becomes final on every app, four settings that were
saved but ignored now work, and every app catches up with the new server.

Changes since **v2.5.1** (2026-10-06): about 130 commits across PRs #101–#121.
The full record, with every API field, is the `[Unreleased]` section of
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Before you upgrade

- **20 migrations (00037–00057; there is no 00053).** Apply them with your
  usual migrate step, or set `AUTO_MIGRATE=true` to apply them at startup
  (see [deployment.md](../deployment.md)). `/health/ready` stays unready until
  they're applied.
- **The next scan does some one-off work:** it turns episodes filed as `E01`
  whose file is really `E01-E02` into one multi-episode item, fills in movie
  editions and multi-part movies from file names, reads featured-artist
  credits, and regroups photos (photos are now told apart by file, not by
  name, and RAW + JPEG pairs become one photo). Nothing is re-probed.
- **Settings that used to do nothing now apply.** If you set any of these
  before, check them:
  - a library's **metadata language** (titles and summaries change on the
    next metadata refresh);
  - a user's **concurrent-stream limit** now counts direct plays, not only
    transcodes;
  - a user's **quality profile** (resolution, video and audio bitrate, codec)
    now converts anything above it, at home too;
  - Settings ▸ Transcode's **encoder tuning** (NVENC preset, tune, rate
    control, peak-bitrate ratio) now overrides `TRANSCODE_NVENC_*` /
    `TRANSCODE_MAXRATE_RATIO` on the server itself, after a restart.
- **The server holds every app to its play decision.** `GET /media/stream`
  refuses a video the server would convert for that viewer (over the internet
  limit from outside the home network, or over their quality profile) with
  409 `TRANSCODE_REQUIRED`, and a damaged file with 422. Current apps never
  hit this. Older app builds that decide on their own lose the original file
  for those viewers, and casting (which sends the original file) fails for a
  video over the viewer's quality profile. Music, audiobooks, books and
  downloads are not affected.
- **The internet streaming limit needs the viewer's real address.** Behind a
  reverse proxy or Cloudflare, list the proxy in `TRUSTED_PROXIES` or every
  viewer looks local.
- **New, optional environment variables:** `ONSCREEN_DVR_POSTPROCESS_SCRIPT`,
  `ONSCREEN_COMSKIP_PATH` / `ONSCREEN_COMSKIP_INI`, `DOWNLOAD_MAX_CONVERSIONS`
  (default 1, max 2), `DOWNLOAD_CACHE_TTL_HOURS` (7 days),
  `DOWNLOAD_CACHE_MAX_GB` (50).
- **New, optional keys in Settings ▸ General:** an OMDb key (IMDb, Rotten
  Tomatoes and Metacritic scores) and a fanart.tv key (clear logos, banners,
  disc art). Without them those features stay empty.
- **Upgrade remote transcode workers too.** Burned-in picture subtitles and
  TrueHD/DTS passthrough only go to workers that support them (v2.6 workers
  and the built-in worker). With only older remote workers, burn-in is
  refused ("This server can't burn in subtitles right now") and TrueHD/DTS
  keep being converted to AAC.
- **New tasks that are off until you enable them:** music loudness analysis,
  photo face detection, chapter-thumbnail backfill, automatic subtitle
  download. Turn them on in Settings ▸ Tasks if you want them.
- **Built with Go 1.26.9 and x/net 0.60.0** (GO-2026-6612, -6613, -6617).

## Highlights

- **Edit metadata, with field locks.** Fix a title, year, rating, genres or
  summary, and refreshes and Fix Match leave your edits alone. Edit up to 500
  titles at once from a library.
- **Multi-episode files, editions and multi-part movies.** `S01E01-E02` is one
  episode covering both; `{edition-Director's Cut}` and friends label versions;
  `cd1` + `cd2` play as one movie on every app.
- **Import from Plex, Jellyfin or Emby.** Watched state, resume points,
  ratings, favorites, playlists and play history, matched by file path, folder
  or provider IDs, with a preview before anything is written.
- **Labels and label restrictions.** Label titles and hide them from (or limit
  a user to) those labels, everywhere the content-rating limit applies.
- **Collections you make.** Hand-picked or rule-based ("80s horror"),
  shown on everyone's home screen, with uploaded covers and Kodi NFO import.
- **Smaller downloads and optimized versions.** Download a 1080p/720p/480p MP4
  instead of the original; pre-convert titles so weak devices and remote
  viewers play a copy directly instead of holding a live transcode.
- **Home vs. internet limits.** A separate bitrate limit for viewers outside
  your network, server-wide and per user.
- **Music:** instant mix, similar artists, featured-artist credits and
  "Appears on", and loudness analysis for files with no ReplayGain tags.
- **Podcasts by RSS** with auto-download and "keep the newest N".
- **Photos:** camera RAW, RAW + JPEG pairs, faces you can name, and "On this
  day" memories.
- **Picture subtitles on every app.** PGS, VobSub and DVB subtitles can be
  burned into the video when an app can't draw them; if an OCR'd text
  version exists, that is used instead, with no re-encode.
- **Audio passthrough on remux.** Android TV / Fire TV tells the server which
  formats its HDMI output takes, and TrueHD, DTS(-HD), E-AC-3 (Atmos) and AC-3
  are copied to the receiver instead of being converted to 5.1 AAC.

## What's new by area

### Library and metadata
- **Metadata editor with field locks** (movies, shows, seasons, episodes),
  plus **bulk edit** for content rating, genres, labels and locks.
- **Multi-episode files** (`S01E01E02`, `S01E01-E02`, `1x01-1x02`, …) shown as
  "E1–E2" and counted as every episode they cover.
- **Episode order: aired, DVD or absolute,** per library with a per-show
  override (TMDB episode groups, TheTVDB orders).
- **Movie editions and multi-part movies** (see Highlights).
- **Studio and network pages,** linked from title pages and browsable per
  library.
- **Clear logos, banners and disc art** from files next to your media, then
  fanart.tv, then AniList banners.
- **IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores** from OMDb, kept within the
  key's daily limit.
- **Save metadata as NFO files** next to your media (off by default) so Kodi,
  Jellyfin and Emby see the same details.
- **Automatic subtitle download** from OpenSubtitles for missing languages
  (off by default).
- **Fix subtitle timing for everyone:** an admin's sync correction is stored on
  the server and applies on every app.
- **Stricter show matching:** a show must match its folder's name and year
  before it's accepted, so "Avatar" no longer becomes the live-action series.
  TVDB/Sonarr qualifiers like "(US)" and "(2005)" are understood.

### Playback and streaming
- **The server's play decision is final** on every app, and the streaming
  routes enforce it.
- **Quality profile** (Account ▸ Playback quality, and in the TV and phone
  apps): maximum resolution, video and audio bitrate, and the codec used when
  converting, applied on every device.
- **Concurrent-stream limit counts direct plays;** apps show "Too many
  streams".
- **Internet streaming limit,** server-wide and per user.
- **Optimized versions** (Mobile 720p, TV 1080p, or original-quality H.264).
- **Chapter thumbnails** in the player's chapter menu and on title pages.
- **Burned-in picture subtitles** (PGS, VobSub, DVB, XSUB): picking one on a
  stream that goes through the server uses its OCR'd text version if there is
  one, otherwise the stream restarts with the subtitle burned in (a full
  re-encode, drawn on the CPU). Off or another track takes it out again.
- **Audio passthrough on remux** for apps that declare it (Android TV / Fire
  TV): TrueHD and DTS are copied into fMP4 segments, AC-3 and E-AC-3 into the
  usual ones, with no 5.1 channel limit. Audio streams also show their profile
  ("DTS-HD MA", "TrueHD") after the next scan.
- **Signed-in devices:** see and sign out each device, or everywhere else.

### Music, books and podcasts
- **Instant mix, similar artists, featured artists, "Appears on".**
- **Loudness analysis** (EBU R128) for tracks without ReplayGain tags.
- **Podcast RSS subscriptions** with auto-download, retention and an hourly
  refresh.
- **Reading position syncs** between devices for EPUB, CBZ and CBR.
- **OPDS catalog** at `/opds/` for KOReader, Moon+ Reader, Panels and others.
- **M3U playlist import and export.**

### Photos
- **Camera RAW** (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF, ORF, RW2, PEF) using the
  embedded JPEG, and **RAW + JPEG pairs** as one photo.
- **Faces and people:** detection built into OnScreen (off by default),
  naming by hand.
- **Memories:** "3 years ago today" on the home screen.

### Live TV and DVR
- **Recording post-processing:** remux, transcode to save space, delete the
  original after checking the new file, or run your own script.
- **Commercial detection** (comskip, or a built-in black-frame + silence
  detector) with **Skip Ad** in the web player, or cut from the file.

### Accounts, sharing and admin
- **Your own sign-in settings on the Account page,** for every user, not
  only admins: change your password (your other devices are signed out),
  turn two-factor sign-in on or off, set preferred audio and subtitle
  languages, and link Last.fm, ListenBrainz or Trakt.
- **Personal API keys** (`osk_…`, full or read-only) for Home Assistant, Kometa
  and scripts; never accepted for sign-in or account changes.
- **Download my data:** a ZIP of everything the server keeps about your
  account.
- **My stats and Year in review** for every user.
- **Digest email,** weekly or monthly, of what was added (needs SMTP).
- **Map a port on the router automatically** (UPnP, PCP, NAT-PMP; off by
  default, with a clear warning).
- **Import from Plex, Jellyfin or Emby** (see Highlights).

## Apps

Every app was updated for the new server features. Store builds follow the
server release.

- **Android TV / Fire TV:** episode ranges ("S1 · E1–E2") and next-episode
  after a range; version picker for editions and multi-part movies; optimized
  versions; "Too many streams"; clear logos, studios and chapter thumbnails;
  Settings ▸ Playback quality; similar artists, "Appears on" and Instant Mix;
  collection rows on Home; refuses damaged files with a message; a direct play
  the device can't read (a very large .mov index, for example) falls back to a
  remux instead of a full transcode and is remembered for next time; artist
  pages' tab heading and D-pad focus fixed; picture subtitles on server
  streams (text version or burned in); audio passthrough to a receiver.
- **Phone:** the same playback and music features, plus converted (smaller)
  downloads, comic reading-position sync, and original downloads from the
  server's download route.
- **Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS) and Xbox:** episode ranges, version picker,
  optimized versions, "Too many streams", logos, studios, chapter thumbnails,
  Playback quality settings, similar artists, "Appears on", Instant mix,
  collection rows on Home, and picture subtitles (text version or burned in).
- **Roku:** plays the server's play decision (it used to keep its own), episode
  ranges, version picker and multi-part movies, clear logos, studios, similar
  artists, "Appears on" and Instant mix; burned-in picture subtitles on server
  streams; switching audio tracks during a server stream now picks the right
  track and keeps the right position. Roku has not yet been tried on a
  device with this release.
- **Web:** everything above; the watch page waits for the server's play
  decision before it starts, and picture subtitles can be shown (text version
  or burned in) on direct play and server streams alike.

## Fixes

- **Settings that were saved but did nothing now work:** library metadata
  language, the concurrent-stream limit (direct plays count), the quality
  profile and the Transcode page's encoder tuning.
- The web app's My Playlists page (M3U import and export, smart playlists) is
  linked from the sidebar; nothing linked to it.
- **Shows auto-matched the wrong series;** they now get the strict match movies
  got in v2.5.0. Existing matches are kept; use Fix Match on a wrong one.
- MP3 files' MusicBrainz IDs and ReplayGain tags (ID3 `TXXX`) are now read.
- `PATCH /collections/{id}` keeps fields the request leaves out.
- Podcast shows emptied by retention disappear without waiting for a scan, and
  episodes get their real length.

## Known limitations

- **Passthrough** hasn't been tried with a receiver yet: whether the Fire TV
  player reads TrueHD and DTS out of the server's fMP4 stream needs a device
  with a TrueHD/DTS-capable receiver.
- **Burn-in** runs on the CPU (the all-GPU pipelines are turned off for that
  session), and starts at the next subtitle after a seek at the latest.
- **Faces are found, not recognised:** people are named by hand.
- **Casting** sends the original file, so a video over the viewer's quality
  profile can't be cast.
- **Converted downloads** keep an embedded subtitle's original timing.
- **Roku** and the Samsung and LG TV apps haven't been checked on hardware for
  this release.
