Descriptions

One line

OnScreen is a free, open-source media server you run yourself, with apps for the web, desktop, phone and nearly every TV platform.

Short

OnScreen is a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) media server for movies, TV, anime, music, audiobooks, podcasts, books and photos. It runs on your own computer or NAS, transcodes on Intel, NVIDIA and AMD GPUs, and has its own apps for the web, Windows and Linux, Android phones (with Android Auto), Android TV / Google TV, Fire TV, LG, Samsung, Roku, Xbox and Meta Quest. There is no paid tier: every feature is in the free server.

Facts

  • Price: free. No subscription, no lifetime pass, no features held back.
  • License: AGPL-3.0 (the LG webOS and Xbox TV apps: Apache-2.0). Source: github.com/CollinJAycock/OnScreen.
  • Current server release: v2.7.0 (November 2026). Docker image ghcr.io/collinjaycock/onscreen, plus Windows and Linux installers.
  • Built on: Go (a single binary), PostgreSQL and Valkey / Redis.
  • Apps: web (built in), Windows and Linux desktop, Amazon Fire TV (in the Appstore), Android phone with Android Auto and Android TV / Google TV (Google Play beta), and LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Roku, Xbox and Meta Quest (in store review).
  • Media: movies, TV, anime (AniList), music, audiobooks, podcasts, books and comics (EPUB, CBZ, CBR), photos, Live TV and DVR.

Things worth showing

  • Dolby Vision done properly: profile 7 (UHD Blu-ray) is converted to profile 8.1 on the fly, so a single-layer Dolby Vision TV shows Dolby Vision instead of HDR10.
  • Hardware transcoding on NVIDIA (NVENC), Intel (QSV / VAAPI) and AMD, including HDR to SDR tone mapping on the GPU, and extra machines can join as transcode workers.
  • Bit-perfect music: gapless playback, ReplayGain, hi-res and DSD, and exclusive-mode output in the desktop app.
  • Android Auto for music, audiobooks and podcasts, and Chromecast casting of any video.
  • The server decides what each device can play (direct play, remux or transcode) and shows why in Now Playing.
  • Single sign-on in the free server: OIDC, SAML and LDAP, plus two-factor sign-in.
